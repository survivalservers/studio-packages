# Get help

> How to reach our team.

Write to us at studio@survivalservers.com, or through [Talk to us](https://studio.survivalservers.com/#talk) on our website, for any question: a higher limit, more own machines, a virus scan you think is wrong, or an uptime claim. We reply within one business day. Send your studio's name, what you did and what you expected, the times in UTC, the region, and the `Request-Id` of any failed answer.
